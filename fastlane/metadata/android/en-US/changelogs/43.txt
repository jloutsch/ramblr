Better cleanup, and much less memory when idle.

- New default cleanup style, Natural: joins broken sentences, drops fillers and keeps your tone, without adding or swapping words. Formal is still available.
- Risky cloud cleanup now falls back to your raw transcript.
- Silent taps say "No speech detected" instead of inserting your vocabulary list.
- On-device models only load when you use on-device transcription, and unload after 5 idle minutes.
